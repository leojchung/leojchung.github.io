I also teach, design curriculum, and spend a great deal of time on art, music and sport, which is less a separate life than the reason the research interests me. Principles

What I care about

Three things that show up in everything above.

Reproducibility

Good science comes from tight execution. I troubleshoot and iterate until an assay works cleanly, not just once.

Clear data

Results only help if people can act on them — I organize and present data so a team can make decisions quickly.

The big picture

Careful work upstream is what makes the data downstream mean something. Knowing where my piece fits matters.
