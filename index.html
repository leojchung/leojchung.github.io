Hi — I'm Leo. I'm a scientist and a teacher at UBC, and most days that means chasing questions about the brain that don't fit in one lab: chromatin and epigenetics on one end, the gut's surprisingly direct line to the brain on the other, and animal communication somewhere in between. Different systems, same habit of mind.

What ties it together is measurement — figuring out what's actually going on inside from whatever you can honestly record. That's also why I care about teaching: I co-created and taught a for-credit UBC seminar on the neuroscience of art, because explaining something well is usually the fastest way to actually understand it.

Outside of that I'm a Vancouver kid — biking, chess, most ball sports, and an unreasonable amount of loyalty to the Lakers and the Rams. More of that on the Fun page.
